Anomalous Energy Transmutation High-Efficiency Reactor (AETHER)

A large centralized reactor located in the bottom floor of the Dei Nadis IOT&C storage facility. It takes in all the unharvested Eostraphon generated by all ARCH systems and converts it into electrical energy at a terrifying scale, generating ~1.2 Quettawatts (QW) at peak performance. While such amounts of energy seem overkill even for a country, the power required to work the technology of the IOT&C is staggering, considering the amount of anomalous objects they deal with daily. After IOT&C use, the remaining ronnawatts are distributed out by various bogus power plants and shell companies working under IOT&C.

The AETHER works by first evaporating Eostraphon into its gaseous state then compressing the gas into a central chamber using high-output particle accelerators. As Eostraphon really does not enjoy being packed very tightly, it causes them to react violently and vibrate at immense speeds before eroding at a rapid rate. This process releases extremely large amounts of electricity while also effectively disposing Eostraphon. The electricity is distributed to the general public under guise of shell companies.

If it is destroyed, we are destroyed. Ah fuck, even if our profit tanks by a bunch- if we have this.. atleast we can start all over again.`
