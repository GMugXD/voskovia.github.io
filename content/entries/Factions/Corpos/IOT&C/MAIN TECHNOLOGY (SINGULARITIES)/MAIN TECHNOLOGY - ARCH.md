`Anomaly Resonance Containment Harvester (ARCH)`

The basis of one of the main resources unique to IOT&C, [[Eostraphon.]]

ARCH is a system which extracts a substance known as "Eostraphon" from the suffering and satisfaction of sentient anomalies. It is typically harvested in fluid form and stored in dynastrona canisters, but it can also take gaseous and crystalline forms.

This substance is harvested due to the high energy output it creates. It is also known for being a powerful hallucinogen in the underground world, primarily being consumed or inhaled.

As such, IOT&C sells this substance as electricity via the use of shell companies, and also sells them as high-grade drugs to street vendors or high-grade clients in forms such as cigars.

`Of course, there are always more ways of making money. We can't just let the anomalies sit there while they're waiting to be auctioned off, that's just a waste of time and profit. What if we harvested their energy while they are still contained by us?`
