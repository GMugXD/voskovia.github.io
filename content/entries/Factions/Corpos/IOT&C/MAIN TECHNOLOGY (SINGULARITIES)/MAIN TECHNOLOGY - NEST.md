`Non-Euclidian Spacial Technology (NEST)`

NEST is a specialized form of space warping (reshaping space itself rather than forming wormholes), which in many cases provides pocket storage space. While other companies provide ways of making storage "bigger on the inside", what NEST aims to do is make a space in which the contents are fully "safe" from the outside world.

`Due to the anomalous nature of the products IOT&C sells, it is paramount that there is a safe and efficient means of transport, while also keeping a low profile so as to not alert authorities.`
