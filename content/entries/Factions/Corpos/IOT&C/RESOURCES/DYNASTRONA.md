`Dynastrona`

An anomalous alloy which is very strong and regenerates almost instantly if damaged. However, the regeneration part of the alloy can be nullified, making it behave similarly to titanium if one recites the chant [REDACTED], and vice-versa. Most of the storage and containment equipment that IOT&C uses is made from this material due to the volatile nature of some anomalies.

This alloy is harvested in mass amounts by automated processes in a dimensional rift somewhere in northeast Voskovia. As a pact with certain Great Houses, the location of the rift is kept highly secret, and the guarders are not told of the existence of the rift. In any case where the existence of the rift is found out, the highest grade ameprims will be administered followed by a routine check of all the guards on-site.
