# IOT&C

**IPHAVAURITA OMPRISO (OMPRYSERNA) TRADING & COMPANY**

>IOT&C is a anomalous group operating in the red light district of Voskovia with the main interest of gaining as much money as possible, no matter the cost.
>
>Trading with any faction, they primarily deal in anomalous objects such as entities, items, or experiences.

# **INFORMATION 

`"Allied only to money."`

Why do rich people care about making money?
The poor must care about doing so to survive. The middle class must do so as part of their culture of dependency.
The rich make money to become richer. They see money not as a resource, but as a score.
They believe themselves to be on top of the world. Unreachable. Untouchable.
As it is with a capitalist system of course, there is always one richer.


There exists a group that contains economic assets beyond belief.
With a glace, they could bankrupt a country. Level a city. Plunge the world into thermonuclear war.
However, they are one of the least volatile of the redlit groups. They bide their time, putting others into check but never mate.
They are the auctioneers of the anomalous. The ones moving the board. The unassailable economic powerhouse. 

They are Iphavaurita Ompriso Trading & Co.

It is important to know that Iphavaurita Trading & Co. do not just sell to the rich.
They do not exist to spice up the life of the rich with new ways to get richer.
They exist to make money. They exist to be richer than anyone else in the world.
Ruthless as they are, Iphavaurita Trading & Co. are willing to sell to any buyer; groups such as rival nations are some of their most valuable clients.
Sometimes their sales lead to a fancy dinner party, other times the suffering of millions. It doesn't matter to them. Why would it?
So long as money is being made, it doesn't matter where it comes from, right?

The critical step is to gain a market monopoly. If you are the only seller in town, you can set the price at any level you desire, so long as you can drum up demand. In this regard, Iphavaurita Trading & Co. have the advantage: for hundreds of years, they were the only peddlers of anomalous wares in the world. They had the time to gain the capital, and with that capital they can now outprice even the most competitive upstart.

The group is not known for the use of force; they prefer to apply extreme financial and political pressure to achieve their goals. When forced to use more direct means, Iphavaurita Trading & Co. employs outside agents, often nameless contractors embedded within hundreds of layers of shell companies.
