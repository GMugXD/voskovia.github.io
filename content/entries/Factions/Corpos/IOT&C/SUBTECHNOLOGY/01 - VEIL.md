`Visual and Electronic Illusionary Lamination (VEIL)`

VEIL is a lamination coating made for anomaly trafficking going through commercial transport.

Made up of quintillions of electrically manipulatable microfibers, it scrambles a person's perception what is inside of a package. VEIL also disrupts X-Ray, ultrasound and radio imaging machines so that they show different contents from what is really inside.

The fibers can be coded to show various different illusions beforehand.

`Using another material found from the quarry, IOT&C used it to solve another blaring problem- Secrecy. Dedicated ships would be too troublesome for IOT&C, as it would just be something new to maintain. Something that could lose them money. Something that is a liability. After all, other ships already exist. Why not just use them? Of course, VEIL is not needed when IOT&C allied ships are transporting the goods. However, most of the operations are done on commercial cargo ships with low costs of transport. Well, as low as it can be.. So in turn, VEIL was implemented to continue using these cheap means of delivery and also to maintain a veil of secrecy.`
