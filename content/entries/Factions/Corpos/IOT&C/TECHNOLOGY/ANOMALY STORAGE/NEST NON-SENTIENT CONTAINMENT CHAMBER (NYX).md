NEST Containment Chamber (Non-Sentient)
Codename: Nyx
Technology: NEST
As with having a large assortment of anomalous objects and entities to sell, there has to be some way to store them. To be as efficient as possible, we've made 2 types of storage units which are capable of easily being shifted around and have it's internal geometry changed.
A cube shaped container which measures 70 square feet, which is variably larger on the inside, from 1x all the way to 6x. This specific container type is meant for storing non-sentient anomalies. More than 1 anomaly can be stored in one of these containers at any given time, with proper labelling and documentations listed outside the door of the container. It is also lined with Dynastrona to prevent breaches or corrosion if the object is dangerous enough. The container is specially designed for easy transportation, such as with on-site cranes.
