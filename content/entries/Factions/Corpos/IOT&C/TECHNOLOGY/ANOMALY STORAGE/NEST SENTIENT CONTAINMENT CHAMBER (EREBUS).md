Codename: Erebus
Technology: NEST, NEURALYZER (2), ARCH (ARGON)
As with having a large assortment of anomalous objects and entities to sell, there has to be some way to store them. To be as efficient as possible, we've made 2 types of storage units which are capable of easily being shifted around and have it's internal geometry changed.
The same as Nyx, however with a few added technologies. An alarm system has been installed incase of a potential anomaly breach which is directly synced up to [FALUGEIS], which will alert any on-site employee of the breach alongside allies to quickly deal with the breach.

An ARGON device is also installed in the corner of the room. On the top of the chamber lies a tank which accumulates generated Eostraphon which is either harvested or approved to be sent to the AETHER.

The front panel also lists the vitals and a short description of the anomaly and their nature.

Erebus chambers are also black on the outside for identification between Nyx chambers.

`Coined the name "Erebus" for the fear factor of knowing there is a  sentient anomaly stored inside.`
