NEST Transport Container
Codename: "Scaffold"
Technology: NEST, VEIL, NEURALYZER 
Sometimes, clients may request to have the item delivered to their doorstep once the auction is over, or they attended the auction digitally. In such cases, a delivery is called. The delivery option only exists as IOT&C can charge customers extra for a delivery, considering the nature of the goods.

For larger anomalies, IOT&C uses standardized shipping containers with NEST technology built in them which increases the interior capacity by 6x. The inner lining of the containers are made with pure dynastrona and is laced with an anomalous sedative that neutralizes any sentient anomalies until they arrive at their destination.

As an extra precaution due to commercial cargo checks, the VEIL system was specifically developed for this case and is implemented into the containers.

`Coined the nickname "Scaffold" for its stack ability, resembling scaffolding.`
