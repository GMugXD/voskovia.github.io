NEST Courier Trunk
Codename: "Trojan"
Technology: [[Voskovia/Entries/Factions/Corpos/IOT&C/MAIN TECHNOLOGY (SINGULARITIES)/MAIN TECHNOLOGY - NEST|NEST]], NEURALYZER 
Sometimes, clients may request to have the item delivered to their doorstep once the auction is over, or they attended the auction digitally. In such cases, a delivery is called. The delivery option only exists as IOT&C can charge customers extra for a delivery, considering the nature of the goods.

For smaller items up to the size of a humanoid, IOT&C equips couriers with their state-of-the-art courier trunk. Visually, it does not bear any suspicion, instead looking just like a regular courier trunk. But the trunk is about 5.4x larger on the inside and is laced with an anomalous sedative that neutralizes any sentient anomaly until they arrive at their destination. The inner lining of the trunk is also made from pure dynastrona. 

`Coined the nickname "Trojan" for its unsuspecting appearance.`
