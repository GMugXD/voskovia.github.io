Houses are the main political, governing entities that make up the basis of the political structure of [[Voskovia]].
- MUST govern territory and their subjects*
- CAN be for profit, and any purpose
- Has the [[Delegated Powers|powers of a house]]
- Recognised as an House.

While the term "House" can be used to all houses, not all houses are equal, with some types of houses being officially recognised while others not. The types of houses include:
- Royal House (V. Voskovia)
- Noble Houses (H. Evanesca)
- Vassal Houses (h. Elysia)
- Directive Houses (O. Taimera)
- Council Houses (C. Iterev)
- Regional Houses (I. Ostria)
    - Vincial, State, Subdist, and Distro Houses
