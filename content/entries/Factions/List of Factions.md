HOUSES
- Voskovia is voskovia.
- Oshiro, Heirta, Alphenim, are the three pillar houses. They are the big three.
- Evanesca is the house that fronts SW voskovia. Aligned with Oshiro.
- Elysia,  Hydrigon, are vassals of Evanesca. 
- Iria is a partner of Evanesca.
- Accledia is the house of law. (accolades)
- Permida is the house of time. (Permida Time Protocol)
- Yunoira has majority hold in the corpo Yunima.
- Valkyria, is both a corpo and house.

CORPOS
- E6 is one of the biggest entertainment providers, being everywhere. Majority hold by House Elysia.
- IOT&C is mirror of BlackRock and lobcorp; specialising in ompryso and other experimental tech. They are one of the most powerful.
- Yunima is the most prominent teahouse chain.
- Invegard is the biggest health, protection and security, and rapid response for hire corp.
- Eranova does it all, and is the most powerful corporation, with its own private military and corporate concordes.
- Valkyria also does it all, but is both simultaneously a house and corpo. On par with Eranova.
- A.R.C. is research and heavy industries, but notable for their energy research.
- Triathlon makes chrome. Really fucking good chrome.
- Opsecte does it all, but does specialise and is known for their security and extremely vicious merc companies. 
- Styrene makes chrome, and heavy industries.

GUILDS
- EXTG or The Extinction Group are terrorists, lead by a high functioning insync. They have their own unique technology 
- FIEND is an unrecognised corpo, since it does literally everything that's illegal. 
- The Chain was one of the strongest criminal merc org, but now lives in the shadow of its former glory.
- The 82nd worship chrome, and have a brutal culture around it.
