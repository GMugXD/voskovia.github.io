Osins, or Organisations, refers to entities like the [[Voskovian Central Military (VCM)|VCM]] which are entities that are recognised but control no land nor work for profit.*
- CANNOT govern territory and their subjects*
- CANNOT be for profit, and MUST be for a purpose
- Has Osin powers (needs to be defined)
- Recognised as an Osin.

With this, they can be described somewhat as recognised guilds. Osins which control and govern territory are referred to as [[Vosins]]
