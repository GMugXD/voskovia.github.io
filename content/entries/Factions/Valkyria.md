> "Noiresa atov sin Alkyria."

The fiercely independent, fire born House and Corpo superpower.

Valkyria is a small, autonomous, and completely independent distro, having partnership status with House Voskovia, with most of their subjects notably being of their race: The Alkyrians.

For much of its history, it has fought with the Voskovian government for its independence and freedom, but recently it has shifted to becoming an economic state powerhouse, and partnering with V. Voskovia in a historic decision, while keeping most if not all of their autonomy.

Valkyria has since been granted special powers and privileges with its status as both one of the most powerful corpos, while simultaneously being a House and Corpo. That, and it's long history of being free from Voskovia and other things has basically made it act more or less just act like an foreign nation in voskovian affairs.

Valkyria is an exonym, while most subjects of Valkyria refer to it as Alkyria. Additionally, their dialect of voskovian is drastically different and is more or less treated as it's own language.
