- All words have their ending vowels reflect a rough meaning of the word.
- -a for abstract concepts,
- -e for processes,
- -i for plural,
- -o for concrete, physical things or entities,
- and -u for the absolute, primordial form of the word

- Ex:
- Vira (Life, health, etc..)
- Vire (Living)
- Viri (Lives, or living things/beings)
- Viro (Living being)
- Viru (Lifeforce itself)

- Note: Vowel ending -u- is not used in regular discussion except for scientific terms about the underlying forces of reality and the world, as well as archaic precursor words.
