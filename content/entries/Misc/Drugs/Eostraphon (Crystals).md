Eostraphon is the substance extracted from anomalies by IOT&C that is widely prized in the underworld for its highly hallucinogenic effects. Being extracted straight from a //REDACTED// force from stored anomalies by the IOT&C, it also possesses highly anomalous properties. 
Such properties include it not being able to harm a person's body, making it theoretically one of the best heavy-hitting drugs with no drawback. The induced "high" state can also apparently "go away" and "reappear" at will within a certain period of time as stated by users.

As a result, Eostraphon has slowly inched its way to becoming the holy grail of all drugs in underground Voskovia.

One of the most common ways are to inhale them as powder. Through a crystallization process carried out by the IOT&C, the liquid Eostraphon is turned into crystals which are then ground up and sent off to dealers or sometimes dealt by IOT&C at their paranormal auctions for free to their guests. The crystals are. described to be light green, with them glowing faintly in the dark.

Of course, the making and real name of Eostraphon has never been made public, and any civilian efforts to find out its origin has been met by quick suppression by IOT&C's many subsidiaries. On the streets, it is known as "Staph".
