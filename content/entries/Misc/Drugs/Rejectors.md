A sometimes necessary drug which numbs-rejects the body's ability to feel emotion, and generally improves focus.

The potency of the rejection varies from drug to drug, but regular users generally taking medium to high potency. 

Unlike other drugs, or drugs in general, the general usage of rejectors are frowned upon and is often taboo, as most rejectors in use are low quality, leading to their effects lingering and often becoming permanent. This can happen with prolonged or excessive use on any type of rejector, aswell as other reasons.
Emotions, feelings, expression, and everything in-between are of the most important qualities in voskovian culture and ideals. A drug that numbs or culls it permanently is understandably taboo.

>Notes:
>- Reject: What used to be the word for people on rejectors, has become an insult for people who act nonchalant and distant without any apparent reason other than to look "cool"
