The name of the legion of self-organised, autonomous force of vessels and constructs that were originally a military protocol (of the same name) spearheaded by H. Alphenim and the [[Voskovian Central Military (VCM)|VCM]], but has since unchained themselves and operates throughout the VHLR, with their signatures being nigh undetectable even by the best intelligence and informatia tech.

They have been known to target key [[Voskovian Central Military (VCM)|VCM]] settlements, grids, stations, infrastructure, vessels, and even personnel. The Spawn utilise the Datcosm to its fullest as intelligences built solely on data, using it to sabotage and disrupt key infrastructure, even outside VCM protocols.
The [[Voskovian Central Military (VCM)|VCM]] believes the Spawn currently possess around 1 concorde of vessels. Spawn forces have been sighted to use new spawn technology (informatia, signatures, etc) and have their own language which is undecipherable by voskovian iri. Spawn vessels appearance vary, but their main forces have complete pitch vantastel hull and a striking red glow when they strike, but most of their vessels are just possessed vessels of voskovian origin, with nothing changed, to blend in with forces, and then to ambush pilots.

The term has since grown to cover any rogue AI that seeks harm, taking control of a vessel, weapon systems, constructs, stations, or sometimes even people, via the Datcosm or other mediums. It is also used as a slur for people with [[Non-Reactive Graft Dysphasia|NRGD]] (Non-Reactive Graft Dysphasia), or hyperchromed individuals in general.

The Spawn's motives are completely unknown, but in recent times (2587c and forward), their presence has been quelled by the joint effort of the [[Voskovian Central Military (VCM)|VCM]] x [[Voskovian Enforcement Division|VED]], with help from the [[Voskovian Intellingence Agency|VIA]]'s special spawn crackdown force "Centrifuge". The occasional ambushes that happens are mostly treated like how car accidents are treated. *Tragic*, but people move on.
