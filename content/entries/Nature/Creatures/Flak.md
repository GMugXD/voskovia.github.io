Cute space machine butterfly-birds. Very small and social machination lifeforms that resemble butterflies, with six wings, two eyes, and mouths with mandibles that produce rudimentary meiological lasers to cut and harvest minerals from erisei, which is their natural environment, but they have also thrived in voskovian settlements and grids, hitching rides on vessels and sometimes eating them, creating those triangular mark textures that their mouths make, and that most vessels which have seen extensive use possess. Grids, specifically forgegrids and similar, are one of the places where they thrive exceptionally well and are deeply welcomed, feeding on excess redundant or useless, subpar fysen.  

The flaks' also produce special flak meiss "enzymes" that reinforce, harden and chemically densify—_contract_—synthetic fysen, when it comes into contact with heat or energy, like it does when they use their lasers to nibble. This literally enhances and reinforces otherwise subpar materials, which are nibbled by a drome of flak. Flak also output by-products and excess minerals, in the form of dark, reflective meiss crystals called fayn, which is extremely desirable and applied in a variety of applications for many reasons.

Their physical maijims that produce rudimentary, focused lasers are the same concept used by VCM lances and erisei mining lasers. Additionally, their appearance is extremely striking, as their wings resemble ymira fragments, and with metallic, almost vantastel for their bodies. Flak bond with voskovians and some voskovians keep entire dromes of flak as pets.
