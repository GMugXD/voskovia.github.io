The formi are socially complex, cuboid creatures, made up of one big cube body and smaller protruding cubes. The cubes (known as cores) are an extremely efficient data storage maijim unique to the formi, instead of relying on the Datcosm. Cores are also used for reasoning and thinking, however this is secondary and only gives a forma a limited intelligence. Formi basically are walking cube GPUs. 
The formi move and act by rolling and jumping.

They vary in size and intelligence. Formi usually bond with other formi, *form*ing a group / colony of formi (known as a formula). They reproduce by fusing and *form*ing a new formi, sharing opinions and interests of the parents as well as all of their memories, leading to generational knowledge over millenia. They develop (growing their cores, gaining more cores) by fusing with matter and converting it into sustenance.

Formi are worldborn, but have spread across worlds by latching onto vessels, and other stellborn creatures, as well as collision and currents. They enter hibernation when in space, and are a stelltraveling hazard as their cores are stupidly strong and can shatter the hulls of vessels at high speeds.

> Note: role in the machisystem and setting at large: Formi exist as a notable "fodder wildlife" within the machisystem. They aren't meant to be too interesting.
