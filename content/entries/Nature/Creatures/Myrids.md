A type of [[Machinations|machination lifeform]] which resembles fearsome worm-squids: slithering, erisei boreing and wandering the [[Stell]]. Their appearance is comparable to [[Strings|strings]]/[[Cables|cables]]; their body being long, while remaining disproportionately thin. The body of the Myrid grows at the ends, becoming smaller and thinner until it becomes the branching multiples of tails at their ends.

They are fearsome hunters, attacking [[Vessels|vessels]] or [[Machinations|machinations]] (even other myrids) to consume their workings and arte, which they sustain [[Pure Energy (Pen)|pen]] from. They aren't particularly smart, but are still highly dangerous because of their method of attack, where they bore into [[Erisen|erisei]], travel with them, and then using their senses, they jump prey. 
Myrids use their body and completely wrap around prey in breakneck speeds, imploding them as they sustain themselves. Their branching, slithering tails are also used to deflect projectiles and defend themselves when needed. 

While myrids like killing eachother, they can also work together, with smaller myrids wrapping around and coiling up with bigger myrids, and dis[[Pure Energy (Pen)|pen]]he [[Pure Energy (Pen)|pen]] they gather, forming Myriads.

Myrids possess no sight, hearing, taste or any of the sort. They only possess the sense of signals, which allows them to sense data signals, communication and anything via the [[Datcosm|datcosm]] as if seeing it.
Additionally, as Myrids cannot die of age, they are effectively immortal unless starved or killed.

The scales of the Myrids make them desirable for some applications, with their tails being effectively indestructible, on par with [[Tantameist]] or other military exclusive composites.
