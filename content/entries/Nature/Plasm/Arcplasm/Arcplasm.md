A subset of the [[Machinations|machination lifeforms]]. They are best described as holographic blocky, machination neoplasm which grows rapidly, harvest and refine [[Pure Energy (Pen)|pure energy (pen)]]energy (pen)]]. Their method of refining and harvesting pen varies from colony to colony, of which Arcplasm possess special meiological components (called meijims) that allow them to run simulations and processing operations, powered by pen to increase efficiency of pen refining, and to develop entire new methods of pen harvesting. 
The identified methods of pen harvesting are known as the arcplasms' Arcs, and utilise extremely meticulous but efficient chains of harvesting, refining mechanical organs (meijims), which are distributed and used to expand their operations and power their simulation meijims. 

Arcplasm colonies can stretch and cover entire pryi and erisei, and can combine and merge with other colonies to form megacolonies that either keep their separate arcplasm colony identity but share processing power and methods they have found, or merge into one colony altogether. This can happen even between different species of arcplasm, which there are many. They also possess a rudimentary method of communication using binary data via the Datcosm, the flow of pen, or other methods. 

They spread to other pryi and erisei by collisions and other celestial events. Arcplasm colonies appear like holographic stretches of metallic dark gray, with their maijims and other processes being tall, almosted pixelated spires. Arcplasm colonies also like to organise themselves, which combined with the aforementioned traits, make colonies resemble massive stretches of three dimensional, living mechanical motherboards/CPUs.
