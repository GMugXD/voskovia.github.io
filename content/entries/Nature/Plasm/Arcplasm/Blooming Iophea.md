A species of [[Arcplasm]] which refines and harvests pen from blood. It is an urban species, meaning it is a species adapted to live and thrive in populated areas, like settlements.
Since their datadiction, voskovians have cultivated and used selection methods (intentionally and accidentally) to accentuate their slight red tint and maijims that resembled spirals, to vivid, dusk red blooming flowerbeds that spread rapidly. With the rapid rate of evolution and innate "innovation" that arcplasm and its species possess, the blood to pen conversion arc would become so efficient that it would see use in military and technological applications by the use of Iöphenim technology.
