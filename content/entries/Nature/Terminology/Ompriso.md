The native term for anomalous entities.
They are rampant in the VHLR, as products of catastrophes tearing reality apart, byproducts of the bygone precursors, or just there for an anomalous, unknown or undefined reason. For whatever reason, and whatever cause, they exist and do what they do. 

They are generally utilised by the VCM, corpos and houses for technology, and other benefits, but their exact jurisdiction falls under Vatwatch. (Voskovian Anomalous Territory Watch)

The dominant corpo in the lead with omprisal technology is IOT&C, but that can of anomalous worms is not the focus of this entry.
