While not lacking in good materials, voskovia's specialty material is Fabrics. 

Most fabrics are made from using a mix of biogenetic engineering and a technology lattice to create cheap, but extremely, extremely effective fabrics that rival the flexibility, endurance of the best materials.

Their strength shows itself with their synergy with other materials, often enhancing and reinforcing materials, and making voskovian composite hulls one of the strongest, yet still light and flexible.
