Aka antimatter.
Used as a core fysen in industrial sectes for its properties, as well as military. It also serves all sectes well in its energy output, making it a dominant energy solution next to Pen, and fusion-fission cyclers. Also used in civilian applications.
Jet is easy to come by and abundant.

Jet is also harnessed *naturally* within the VHLR machisystem, predominantly as a means to defend or attack (like how organisms use acid), and also with some machinations using it as an alternative source of sustenance over Pen.

>Note: Notably, jet interacts with spacefabric in a way that makes it pitch black. This is where the name jet is derived from, as in jet-black.
