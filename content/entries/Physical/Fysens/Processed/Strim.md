The refined but raw version of the material used for making strings for stringweaving gear/tech.
An ultralight material which can easily be interfaced, made of up billions of nanofibers interlocked with one another.

Most ultralight vfabrics (also referred to as strim fabrics) use Strim as a basis and add on to it. Neostrim for instance, is a extremely versatile and widely used vfabric, which is just strim that's interfaced.
