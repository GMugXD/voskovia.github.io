Pen is the purest form of energy that is used as the main form of sustunance by many of the VHLR lifeforms, mostly by machination lifeforms. It is also used in the VCM's naval and military technology, notably in the lance, and other applications.

There are many methods of obtaining large amounts of pen, by using natural processes (ie: arcplasm farms) or technological processes, involving very complicated science stuff™.
Despite this, there is a surplus of pen.

Pen can be used by some chrome maijims to power/overcharge them. Arte maijims can accept pen, but can't actually use it.
Pen can be made to take on many different forms, like in the form of liquid, which is used in cocktails.
