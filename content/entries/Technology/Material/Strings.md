Much or less just cables, cableweaving, and cableweaving gear, but using ultralight vfabrics (sometimes called strim fabrics), and cutting most of the capabilities to focus solely on utility and versatility. 

Both cables and strings have their niches. Cables do alot, while Strings focus solely on utility and versatility, completely disregarding mobility. In a way, they are extremely specialised and light cables.

Chromes like SWG (Stringweaving Gear) is especially dominant, being used just as much as CWG (Cableweaving Gear) in personnel, and subjects. Stringweaving is rare in naval applications however, as Cables are more suited.
